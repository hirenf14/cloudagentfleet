# Code of conduct

This project is for people who build and run agent tooling together. We want
it to be a place where anyone can ask questions, report problems, and propose
changes without being talked down to.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Disagree with the idea, not the
  person.
- Be specific and constructive in reviews and bug reports: say what you saw,
  what you expected, and what would help.
- Welcome newcomers, and answer plainly instead of gatekeeping.
- Respect other people's time and privacy. Do not share someone's private
  information, and do not paste secrets, tokens, or machine names into issues.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, insults, discrimination, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Sexualized language or unwelcome attention.
- Deliberately disruptive behavior: spam, trolling, or repeatedly derailing a
  discussion.
- Publishing a vulnerability before the maintainers could fix it (see
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)).

## Enforcement

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and any space that
represents the project. The maintainers may edit or remove comments, lock a
thread, and block people who do not follow it, and will explain the reason
when they can.

To report a problem, use the private
[security advisory form](https://github.com/hirenf14/cloudagentfleet/security/advisories/new)
(choose "other" and describe the conduct issue) or contact a maintainer
through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled in confidence.
