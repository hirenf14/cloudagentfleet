A calmer dashboard, in the style of Open WebUI Computer: black surfaces, hairline borders, 12px type with monospace details, and colour only for status. The top bar is gone; the sidebar holds the brand, live status, search, Recent, Machines, and a new **Sign out**. A machine's page is a short column of Start / Continue / Recent / Details. The sidebar stays usable with many machines and sessions: search shows every match, machines are capped at six (online first, with **Show all**), each machine's sessions fold away, running sessions show three at a time, and ended ones fold into one line. The sign-in page and Hub notices match.
